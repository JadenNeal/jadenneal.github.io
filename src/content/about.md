I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), advised by [Prof. Yanlin Zhang](https://zhyanlin.github.io) and [Prof. Jiaheng Wei](https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/jiahengwei). Before joining HKUST(GZ), I received my M.Eng. in Electronic and Communication Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2023, advised by [Prof. Xingjun Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4Gk6gSIAAAAJ&hl=en), and my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from the University of Science and Technology Beijing in 2020. I also closely collaborate with [Shuo Yang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=IBwxJGEAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Donglin Xie](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=1WKUrC0AAAAJ&hl=en).

My research focuses on the intersection of large language models and biomedicine, spanning genome language models (gLMs), multimodal models, digital-health algorithms, and LLM post-training. Recently, I have been particularly interested in multimodal modeling of biological sequences and natural language, and AI agents for Science and healthcare.
