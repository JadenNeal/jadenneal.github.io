I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), advised by [Prof. Yanlin Zhang](https://zhyanlin.github.io) and [Prof. Jiaheng Wei](https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/jiahengwei). I expect to graduate in December 2027. Before joining HKUST(GZ), I received my M.Eng. in Electronic and Communication Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2023, advised by [Prof. Xingjun Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4Gk6gSIAAAAJ&hl=en), and my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from the University of Science and Technology Beijing in 2020.

My research focuses on biomedical large language models, agentic post-training, and LLM benchmarks. I study how to train models to reason from the evidence available to them and how to measure where that reasoning breaks down. My genomics work includes genome language models and [CoLoopDB](https://coloopdb.nucleome.ai/), a chromatin-loop database that links each result to its supporting contact maps. At HONOR, I worked on cuffless blood-pressure estimation for wearable devices.
