I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), advised by [Prof. Yanlin Zhang](https://zhyanlin.github.io) and [Prof. Jiaheng Wei](https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/jiahengwei). Before joining HKUST(GZ), I received my M.Eng. in Electronic and Communication Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2023, advised by Professor [Xingjun Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4Gk6gSIAAAAJ&hl=en), and my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from the University of Science and Technology Beijing in 2020.

My research focuses on the intersection of large language models and biomedicine, spanning genome language models (gLMs), multimodal models, digital-health algorithms, and LLM post-training. Recently, I have been particularly interested in multimodal modeling of biological sequences and natural language, learning from heterogeneous biological sequence data, LLM post-training through supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, and AI agents for automated model adaptation in AI for Science and healthcare.
