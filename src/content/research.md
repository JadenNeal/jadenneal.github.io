---
title: "Research Interests"
---

- **Biomedical LLMs.** Domain-specific pretraining for medicine and genomics, with an emphasis on data quality and traceable evidence.
- **Agentic Post-Training.** Supervised fine-tuning, preference alignment, and on-policy distillation for models that reason and use tools in multi-step workflows.
- **LLM Benchmark.** Domain-specific benchmarks and error analysis for evidence use, factuality, and reasoning.
